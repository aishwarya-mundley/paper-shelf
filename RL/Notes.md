### Era of Experience
- Streams of knowledge instead of small context answers.
- Agents that think really long term - take detrimental step in the short term which is beneficial in longer run.
- Take input from all types of sensory cues in environment and not just human data.
- Can observe and act as humans & take machine friendly actions that execute code, etc.
- Reward structure will move from being human-centric to more grounded reward - signals that arise from environment itself.
- The reason is human-centric rewards are not grounded in reality. Human feedback is limited to the window of human context which may not comprehend all the facets of environment.
- Bi-level optimization - optimizes human feedback as top-level goal (measure of effectiveness) and optimizes ground signals from the environment at low level. Incrementally improving ground signals will eventually improve human feedback as well.
- In this way experience and human data amalgamate.
- LLMs imitating human chain of thought might not be the most optimal way of universal computer. Other non-human languages might provide better and faster results.
- RL agent can discover its own approaches to solve a problem by being grounded in experience. (Eg : AlphaProof)
- Simulation - closed problem with singular, precisely defined rewards ; Reality - open-ended problems with pluralityof seemingly ill-defined rewards.
- RLHF - removed the need for value functions by utilizing human experts instead of machine-estimated values. Strong & vast human data reduced need for exploration. Human-centric reasoning lessened the need for world model and temporal abstraction.
- Short episodes of ungrounded human interaction not suitable for long streams of grounded, autonomous interaction.
- The black box will grow darker and it'll be difficult to interpret future AI systems.
- Since it is grounded in reality, just like human goal setting there is no guarantee of perfect alignment.
